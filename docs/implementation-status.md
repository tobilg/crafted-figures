# Implementation status

**Current status, 0.8.0.** A new `illustrated` look, because correctness had been the only goal and a figure also has to be worth looking at. No existing preset's geometry or colors change. After the cleanup below, all 24 release groups and 94 unit tests passed at Node 24.22.1 on macOS arm64, using installed Chrome 156.0.8078.74, because the pinned Chromium was not installed. Hosted CI has not run on 0.8.0.

- **Preset and finish.** `illustrated` uses a paper page, one heavy ink outline, and a schema 2 `finish` setting: `contour`, `groundShadow`, `shadowLength`, `light:"view"` and `shading:"smooth"`. There are eight new enamel materials, and `materials.colors` names more from one color.
- **Smooth shading.** Curves and rounded corners sit on a 25-step ramp through the four side tones. Their `side-N` class still names the tone the independent check expects. Upright sides get a soft falloff on screen, and `box` corners get up to 32 facets as their radius grows.
- **Shadows.** Parts cast onto the ground plane, and a shadow stops at the edge of the surface it lands on. Flat tops of at least about 700 square drawing units also receive shadows from what stands above them. These shadows are ignored by the painted-order checks and stay vector in print.
- **Numbered callouts.** `kit.illustration.callouts(stage, items, {pose})` draws numbered badges with leaders and a matching legend in every output, keeps leaders on anchors as parts move, lights up active parts, and places badges automatically against the rest pose. Live badges stay 22 CSS px across at any width, so they remain legible on a phone. `check` fails on `CALLOUT_OVERLAP`, `CALLOUT_OUTSIDE` and `CALLOUT_HIDDEN`, at 390 and 960 px and in every swept state. Both trial figures were converted in scratch copies, dropping their hand-written callout code, and pass `check` with automatic placement, after four anchors were declared on the parts they label and one real occlusion was marked `mayHide`. That conversion also exposed a stale reference when `replacePart` swaps a part, so `part` now accepts a name that is looked up each time. A sheet's own `parts` replace callouts in print; printing a figure with callouts and no `parts` was not exercised.
- **No hand-drawn filter.** It was built and then removed, because `feDisplacementMap` left lines shaky and aliased and cannot antialias. The user caught this, not the checks.
- **Guidance.** `craft.md` now starts with how the object really works, then what makes a figure worth looking at, and ends with questions no check can answer. `SKILL.md` steers everyday objects to the preset. The `kitchen-scale` starter shows all of it.
- **Fixes from two fresh-agent trials.** See [the trial record](../evals/illustrated-trial.md), which also lists what was not fixed. A helper for figures with a state machine and a clock is the clearest remaining gap.
- **New evidence.** `tests/illustrated-browser.mjs` is the `illustrated` release group, with 28 cases: four figures in both themes at 390 and 960 px with tone, order and z-buffer checks, plus callouts at both widths, an overlap that must fail `check`, `audit`, `check` and `drive` on the starter, playback over a state scene, and PDFs with no raster images. `tests/illustrated.test.mjs` and `tests/callouts.test.mjs` add 28 unit tests, and `materials` now covers the preset, for 36 contexts.
- **Cost.** Files grow: the trial toaster went from 0.88 to 1.31 MB and the espresso machine from 1.27 to 2.04 MB. Moving four figures of 10 to 215 parts kept the median frame at 16.7 ms with smooth shading on and off. The worst 95th percentile was 31 ms with it on and 24 ms with it off, on the 90-part starter, on an M2 with no phone measured. This is a record, not a gate; the timing budget stays deferred.
- **License.** The project is now under the MIT [LICENSE](../LICENSE), which ships in the skill package and is declared in both package manifests and the skill's frontmatter; a unit test keeps the two copies identical.
- **Cleanup.** At the owner's request the repository now stands on its own. The early Three.js prototype, the reference submodule, its license notice and every mention of it, the old requirements document and implementation prompts, the historical reviews, increment reports and trial records, and the parity gate that waited on an archive of old trials are removed. So are the regression fixtures derived from that reference and the `source-migration` release group that ran them, leaving 24 groups. A short [specification](spec.md) replaces the old requirements document. Build manifests no longer name a design reference. Everything removed stays in Git history at `b4c3cd5`.
- **Not done.** Safari and Firefox were not run, and `color-mix` needs Chrome 111, Safari 16.2 or Firefox 113 or later.

**0.7.2.** A cleanup with one product fix, smaller print files. The figure, model, configuration and print contracts are unchanged. All 24 release groups and 67 unit tests passed on pinned Chromium 156.0.8078.4, at Node 24.22.1 on macOS. The workflow changes below have not run on hosted CI yet, and the open items in the 0.7.1 entry are unchanged.

- **Print size.** In the exploded assembly's poster, 51.5 of 53.9 MB of uncompressed page content was 5 clip outlines repeated 32–64 times each, because every piece of a smooth surface clipped to the tessellation of its whole group. `outlineOf` in `kit/three/renderer.mjs` now keeps only the outer edges. On the real clips that took 32,564 segments to 1,067, with no winding-number mismatch at 24,000 random points. The print suite's 24 PDFs went from 362.3 to 30.5 MB and the largest from 50.6 to 3.5 MB, and that drawing's HTML from 10.7 to 7.9 MB. Rendered with pdf.js at 2–3×, 9,859 of 103,163,616 pixels across all 32 pages differ, one of them by more than 16/255, and the largest difference is 19/255. The dial has no clips, and none of its pixels changed. A 5× crop of the assembly looks identical before and after. Dropping off-crop paths from detail views was considered first and is not needed. The print suite now fails on an unmerged clip edge and records PDF and HTML sizes.
- **CI artifacts.** A passing run keeps its reports and logs, and any other run also keeps screenshots and outputs. The candidate artifact is now only the ZIP, and the no-install jobs unpack it with `scripts/unpack-release.mjs`, whose output was byte-identical to the assembled folder when checked locally. `test:bundled` no longer repeats the Ubuntu/Node 22.13.0 job, and `test:compat` no longer repeats `npm test` and the consumer type check, so its report has three steps. A passing run should fall from 118 MB to about 14 MB, which is an estimate until a hosted run measures it. The workflow was parsed as YAML, but actionlint was not available for this change.
- **Tests.** `tests/report.mjs` now holds the report lifecycle, browser launch and process runner, used by 34 checks, and duplicated runs of six or more lines fell from 59 to 21. Every browser check uses the skill's own browser discovery and records which browser ran, so the root `playwright` and unused root `vite` dependencies are gone. With no pinned build, 16 of the 24 groups also passed on installed Chrome 156.0.8078.74 during this work, after the two Next checks learned to ignore the favicon request a full browser makes. The other eight groups ran only on the pinned build. `test:source-package` now handles a tracked file deleted before a commit.
- **Smaller surface and docs.** Eight exports that only their own file used are private, and the `launchBrowser` alias is gone; none was reachable through the package's `exports`.

**0.7.1.** Automatic browser discovery and a depth-buffer order check are in, plane-based ordering was declined with evidence, and a lazy-context bug found by hosted CI is fixed. Plane-based ordering is declined because ray sampling, with extra samples on thin rims, closed the one fault the depth check found, and curved parts would need rays anyway. Hosted CI [run 41189536149](https://github.com/tobilg/crafted-figures/actions/runs/41189536149) of `2225bf8` passed all 11 jobs, including the no-install package on Windows at Node 22.13.0 and 24. CI now runs on pushes to `main`, pull requests and manual dispatch, so a branch with no PR is tested only when dispatched. The owner made the repository public on 2026-10-31, and the [public-release audit](public-release-audit.md) found no credentials in its history. Open at the time: fresh agent trials (none since 0.3.x); browser checks and printing on Windows, which no job runs; other browsers and hardware GPUs; and a license. The numeric timing budget remains deferred.
