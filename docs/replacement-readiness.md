Three.js owns the scene hierarchy, geometry and orthographic camera. The packaged illustration renderer owns SVG tones, outlines and painter ordering; matching depth passes support WebGL effects. The [bounded experiment](three-prototype-report.md) and [M3 migration](three-migration-report.md) remain separate historical evidence, excluded from the installable package.

The supported replacement covers authored isometric figures, static/live geometry, materials, motion, bounded effects, diagnostics, React/Next integration, printing and local sharing. It is not source/API drop-in compatibility with Anatomy. Arbitrary imported meshes, free perspective orbit, general transparent sorting, Boolean CAD, universal occlusion correctness and automatic shader conversion remain outside scope. C17/C18 deliberately omit upstream account/gallery/session coupling and rigid aesthetic mandates.

Checks have finite geometry/camera/state envelopes. Independent tone checks cover flat native faces and analytic profiles; swept-route shading uses geometry/facing fixtures and visual review. Subpixel edge and adjacent analytic-band exclusions are narrow and reported. Static print seam treatment applies through the injected kit; separately imported custom renderers need their own print qualification. Effects use explicit vector fallbacks and bounded print rasters. Dense changing geometry remains a material responsiveness limit; [capacity](capacity.md) records measurements without attributing them solely to local hardware or promising a frame rate.
